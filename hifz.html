Tahfeez-ul-Quran Program

A systematic and spiritual approach to memorizing the Holy Quran.

Curriculum Highlights

  • Complete memorization of 30 Juz with proper Tajweed.
  • Daily revision (Dour) to solidify memorized portions.
  • Weekly tests to monitor progress.
  • Basic Islamic Fiqh and Aqaid integration.
  • Tarbiyah sessions focusing on Sunnah and Adab.

Duration & Levels

The standard duration for the Hifz program is 3 to 4 years, divided into several levels based on the student's capacity and progress:

  • Nazira: Fluent reading focusing on Makharij.
  • Level 1 (Foundation): Memorization of the first 5 Juz.
  • Level 2 (Intermediate): Up to 15 Juz.
  • Level 3 (Advanced): Completion of 30 Juz.
  • Takmeel: Final revision and certification.
