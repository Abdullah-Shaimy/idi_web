About the Institute

Nurturing Faith, Knowledge & Character

Comprehensive Hifz Program

A systematic, 4-year curriculum designed to ensure accurate recitation, steady memorization, and lifelong retention of the Holy Quran.

1. Nazira & Tajweed

Mastery of correct pronunciation (Makharij) and complete Tajweed rules to ensure fluent, beautiful recitation before memorization begins.

2. Sabak & Dour System

Daily new lesson memorization (Sabak) combined with systematic revision of recent portions (Sabki) and cumulative revision cycles (Dour).

3. Takmeel & Certification

Complete consolidation of the entire 30 Juz with 3 rounds of Dawrah review, comprehensive testing, and formal certification as a Hafiz.
