About Our Institute

A legacy of Quranic education and spiritual nurturing.

Our History

Established with the noble intention of preserving the words of Allah in the hearts of the believers, the Institute of Da'wa Islamiyya has been a beacon of light for years. We started with a small group of dedicated students and have since grown into a comprehensive institution for Hifz and Islamic sciences.

Meet Our Management

Our Mission

To produce Huffaz who are not only fluent in the recitation and memorization of the Quran but who also embody its teachings in their character and daily lives.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as a premier institution of Islamic education, fostering an environment where tradition meets contemporary understanding.

Founder's Message

"The best among you are those who learn the Quran and teach it. Our aim is to facilitate this noble journey for the upcoming generation, ensuring they become torchbearers of truth and compassion in society."
