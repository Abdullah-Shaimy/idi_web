About the Institute

Our Mission

Nurturing the Next Generation of Scholars

Founded with the vision of providing a serene and focused environment for Hifz, the Institute of Da'wa Islamiyya has been a beacon of light for students seeking to memorize the Holy Quran.

We believe that Hifz is not just about memorization, but about building a deep, spiritual connection with the words of Allah (SWT) and implementing them in daily life through refined Akhlaq.
