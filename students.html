Student Life

A balanced environment of discipline, spirituality, and brotherhood.

Daily Routine

4:30 AM

Tahajjud & Fajr

Students wake up for Tahajjud prayers followed by Fajr congregation and morning Adhkar.

6:00 AM

Sabaq (New Lesson)

Memorization of new portions of the Quran with teachers.

8:30 AM

Breakfast & Rest

Nutritious breakfast and a short break to refresh.

10:00 AM

Sabqi & Manzil (Revision)

Revision of recent lessons and past Juz.

1:00 PM

Zuhr & Lunch

Congregational prayer, followed by lunch and Qailulah (midday nap).

4:30 PM

Asr & Physical Activity

Asr prayer followed by sports and recreational activities.
