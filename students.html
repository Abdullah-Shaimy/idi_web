Life at IDI

Student Journey

A balanced environment of discipline, spirituality, and brotherhood.

Routine

A Day of Devotion

4:30 AM

Tahajjud & Fajr

The day begins with congregational Tahajjud and Fajr prayers, followed by morning Adhkar to set a spiritual tone.

6:00 AM

Sabaq (New Lesson)

Early morning focus is dedicated to memorizing new portions of the Quran with personalized teacher guidance.

10:00 AM

Revision Sessions

Mid-morning hours are spent in 'Dour' (revision) to solidify previously memorized Juz and ensure retention.

1:30 PM

Rest & Nutrition

Zuhr prayer is followed by a nutritious lunch and a period of 'Qailulah' (midday nap) for physical recovery.

4:30 PM

Physical Activity

After Asr, students engage in recreational sports and physical activities to maintain a healthy body and mind.

IDI Madrasa

A center of excellence for Quranic memorization and Islamic education, nurturing the next generation of scholars.
