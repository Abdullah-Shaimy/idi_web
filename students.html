Life at IDI

Student Journey

A balanced environment of discipline, spirituality, and brotherhood.

Routine

A Day of Devotion

4:30 AM

Wake up & Prep

The day begins with early morning preparation followed by the Subah (Fajr) prayer and a refreshing morning tea.

5:25 AM

Sabaq (New Lesson)

Focus is dedicated to memorizing new portions of the Quran with personalized teacher guidance until cleaning sessions start at 6:30 AM.

7:30 AM

Academy Hours

Structured classroom learning and academic pursuits continue until 2:00 PM, followed by personal hygiene and Zuhr prayer.

2:30 PM

Nutrition & Rest

Students enjoy a balanced lunch followed by a compulsory period of 'Qailulah' (rest) to recharge for the evening sessions.

4:10 PM

Dour (Revision)

After Asr prayer, the focus shifts to 'Dour'—systematic revision of previously memorized portions to ensure perfect retention.

6:30 PM

Quran Memorization

Evening sessions after Maghrib are dedicated to intense Quran memorization and self-study until Isha prayer and dinner.

10:00 PM

Night Rest

The day concludes with compulsory rest, ensuring students are well-rested for the next morning's spiritual journey.
