Routine

A Day of Devotion

Explore the disciplined and spiritual daily journey of our students across different schedules.

4:30 AM

Wake up & Prep

Early morning preparation followed by the Subah (Fajr) prayer and a refreshing morning tea.

5:25 AM

Sabaq (New Lesson)

Focused Quran memorization until 6:30 AM, followed by cleaning sessions and academy preparation.

7:30 AM

Academy Hours

Academic classes and structured learning until 2:00 PM, followed by Zuhr prayer preparation.

2:30 PM

Lunch & Rest

Nutritious lunch followed by compulsory midday rest (Qailulah) to recharge for evening study.

4:10 PM

Dour (Revision)

After Asr, systematic revision of previously memorized portions to ensure long-term retention.

6:30 PM

Quran Session

Post-Maghrib memorization and self-study sessions continuing until Isha prayer and dinner.

10:00 PM

Night Rest

The day concludes with compulsory rest for a fresh start the next morning.

4:30 AM

Early Morning

Wake up, Subah prayer, and morning Sabaq session until school starts at 7:30 AM.

11:30 AM

Jumu'ah Prep

Special preparation for Friday Jumu'ah prayer, followed by the Khutbah and congregational prayer.

1:30 PM

Friday Lunch

Traditional Friday lunch followed by a period of rest and relaxation.

4:00 PM

Parents Meeting

Dedicated time for parents to visit and meet with faculty and students.

7:30 PM

Isha & Dinner

Evening prayers followed by dinner and early rest for the weekend.

5:25 AM

Weekend Sabaq

Weekend morning memorization sessions followed by breakfast at 8:00 AM.

8:30 AM

Detailed Dour

Extended revision sessions for solidifying all previously learned Quranic portions.

11:00 AM

Arabic Class

Dedicated hours for Arabic language study and linguistic development.

1:15 PM

Rest & Recreation

Afternoon rest followed by Asr prayer and revision at 4:15 PM.

6:30 PM

Memorization

Evening session for new lessons until dinner and sleep prep at 9:30 PM.
